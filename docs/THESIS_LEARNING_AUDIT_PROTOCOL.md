execution uses new scenario seeds; it does not revise the original experiments or

## Outcomes, reconstruction and uncertainty

Report the complete canonical scalar metrics and duration metrics, five locked reward
components, critical shortfall/fallback counters, unmet critical MWh, and final seven
locality service ratios. Add final minimum locality service and final locality range.
Independently reconstruct all 20 canonical scalar metrics from each saved-in-memory
trace using CUDA float64 arithmetic. This verifies metric formulas, not the simulator
or physical assumptions. Allow at most 0.01 MWh energy discrepancy, 0.00005 daily-reward
discrepancy, and 0.000005 on other scalar metrics against float32 canonical arithmetic.
Keep the existing 0.001 MW power tolerance and reward decomposition tolerance 0.00005.
Any failed identity/contract aborts publication; adverse valid outcomes are retained.

The central estimand is the equal-run, equal-condition mean of selected PPO minus
same-run BC. This describes the fine-tuning plus validation-selection stage relative
to its fixed starting actor, including the extra computation. It does not isolate PPO
from checkpoint selection or additional optimization effort, establish learning from
scratch, or compare against further imitation at an equal budget.

The four PPO-minus-deterministic contrasts are replication diagnostics. Preserve the
original primary criteria and their failed gates; introduce no new binary success
criterion. All intervals are descriptive pointwise nominal 95% intervals, not
familywise statements that all comparisons improve. Report every run and severity,
including negative results, energy/utilization costs and increased switching.

Use 10,000 paired hierarchical resamples, seed 20261031, reusing the frozen CUDA/CuPy
bootstrap. Resample the five paired run indices and the 32 base draws within each of
ten seed strata. Use the same sampled scenario indices across runs and methods;
retain all three coupled shortage variants per draw. Report overall hierarchical,
per-run conditional and severity-specific intervals with linear 2.5/97.5 percentiles.
The severity summaries use 320 conditions each. Means weight all runs/conditions
equally. Five runs remain a small sample; interval coverage is nominal.
